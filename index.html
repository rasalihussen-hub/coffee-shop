OUR HERITAGE & PASSION

Celebrating Ethiopian Coffee Culture

At Alush Coffee, we take pride in serving the world's most aromatic beans right from their ancestral home. Coffee is an essential Ethiopian tradition—a welcoming ceremony, a moment of friendship, and our proudest gift to the world.

From slow-roasted Yirgacheffe and floral Sidama extractions to rich modern macchiatos, our baristas craft each pour with passion and reverence.

  • Traditional Jebena Buna ceremony with frankincense aroma
  • Payment accepted via Telebirr, CBE Birr & cash
  • Fresh daily pastries, Habesha cakes & crispy sambusas
